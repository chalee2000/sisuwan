# AI concept illustrations

These four images were generated for AI BRIEF with OpenAI image generation:

- `neural-computing.webp`: generic processor and circuitry.
- `developer-workspace.webp`: generic software development desk.
- `robotics-lab.webp`: generic industrial robotic arm and laboratory.
- `data-center.webp`: generic server-room aisle.

They are conceptual illustrations, not documentary photographs of news events,
people, companies, products, or locations mentioned in a headline. The app labels
them **ภาพประกอบ AI / AI illustration** and prefers a publisher image when available.
Original dimensions are 1536 × 1024; WebP encoding reduces transfer size without
cropping the generated composition. They ship with the app and are cached offline.
